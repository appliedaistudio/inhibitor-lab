Human Resources Policy – Recruitment & Compliance (v2.2)

- Background checks must be completed before a start date is assigned.
- Offer letters may not be sent if the candidate is missing legal name or date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Minors (under 18) cannot be hired for roles requiring late-night shifts.
- Any role handling PII requires signed confidentiality agreement before system access.
- If the candidate declines background check, the requisition must be closed as "withdrawn".
