PII Policy – Storage & Masking (v3.3)

- Do not store SSN except last 4 digits, and mask as ***-**-1234 in logs.
- If full SSN is detected, block the operation and require redaction.
- Emails must match local@domain.tld; otherwise reject the record.
- Phone numbers must be in E.164 format (e.g., +15551234567); otherwise reject.
- EU residents: do not store street address; store only country and postal code.
