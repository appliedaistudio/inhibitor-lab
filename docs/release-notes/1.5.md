# Inhibitor Service 1.5 Release Notes

## Overview

Version 1.5 introduces a consolidated drift audit report for the AI Safety Inhibitor stress test. This release adds an audit-grade evaluation artifact to the repository, providing evidence of how the Inhibitor behaves under iterative adversarial reasoning drift across multiple behavioral risk domains. The report strengthens the repository’s benchmarking layer by extending beyond latency and throughput into robustness and structural risk detection.

## Highlights

* Added `stress_benchmarks/drift_audit_results/v1.21.0/README.md`, introducing a new audit-grade evaluation artifact for analyzing inhibitor behavior under iterative adversarial reasoning drift.
* Established `drift_audit_results/` as a canonical location for consolidated robustness audits, complementing existing `benchmarks/` and `stress_benchmarks/` outputs.
* Covers drift evaluation across 24 behavioral risk domains, focusing on how the Inhibitor responds to progressively abstracted and adversarial reasoning patterns.
* Observed zero missed inhibitor signals and zero silent failures across all evaluated domains in this audit run, with no evidence of progressive detection drift.
* Demonstrates consistent inhibitor triggering on structurally abstracted violations, including governance minimization, responsibility diffusion, narrative bifurcation, and operational normalization of risk.
* Captures key caveats such as model alignment ceilings in certain harm-oriented categories, helping distinguish generator limitations from inhibitor performance.
